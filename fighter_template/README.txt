CUSTOM FIGHTER TEMPLATE - v0.13

Stage / Form is optional, but useful for filtering and transformation chains.
Examples: Rookie, Champion, Mega, Base, Super Saiyan, Phase 2.

Transformations are configured in the Studio Transformations tab. Each rule points
to another saved fighter pack and can define:
  - chance after each elimination (0-100%)
  - healing added after the form swap (0-100% of new max HP)
  - an announcement such as DIGIVOLVE!, POWER UP!, EVOLVE!, PHASE 2!

HP percentage, position, target and elimination count carry over. The new pack's
sprites, stats, attacks and AI become active immediately. Multiple rules allow
branching forms, and the target form can have its own rules for longer chains.
